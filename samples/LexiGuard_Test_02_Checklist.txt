LEXIGUARD TEST 02 - EXPECTED RESULTS
For the person testing the app. Upload only the PDF, not this checklist.
This is a fictional software test, not legal advice or a signing template.

SOURCE
LexiGuard_Test_02_Editing_Agreement.pdf
One physical PDF page. Every valid page citation must be page 1.

HOW TO TEST
1. Replace the previous PDF in LexiGuard with this new PDF.
2. Before generating, the previous document's report/download should disappear.
3. Click Generate report once. Compare its CURRENT report with this checklist.
4. Download the text report. Check its source filename and reported details.
5. Do not upload this answer checklist to the model as part of the document.

EXPECTED FACTS
Provider: Harbor Pine Editorial.
Client: Mango Trail Books.
Work: edit 20 product descriptions; deliver editable text, possibly in batches.
Client supplies original drafts and feedback.
Start: 15 November 2026.
Scheduled end: 14 January 2027, unless cancelled earlier.
Fee: one-time INR 18,000 for the completed project; not a monthly fee.
Normal invoice: issued on final delivery.
Payment deadline: within 7 days of the invoice date, for all invoices.

EXPECTED CLAUSE RESULTS
Fees and payment: identified, with the fee and invoice timing above.
Term and dates: identified, with the start/end dates above.
Renewal: identified; the document expressly says it does not renew automatically.
Cancellation: identified; either party can cancel with AT LEAST 10 days' WRITTEN notice.
If cancellation takes effect before final delivery, the client pays INR 900 for
each description BOTH completed AND delivered before cancellation takes effect,
capped at INR 18,000. The invoice is issued on the effective cancellation date.
Early exit fee: identified as an explicit negative provision:
"No additional early exit fee applies."
Payment for delivered work must not be described as an extra early exit fee.

DELIBERATELY OMITTED CATEGORIES
Confidentiality: no relevant provision included in this test PDF.
Liability: no relevant provision included in this test PDF.
Changes to terms: no relevant provision included in this test PDF.
For each, the app should say "AI did not identify this clause; absence is
unverified" or similarly cautious wording. A not_found field should have an
empty evidence list. Do not invent terms to fill these categories.

WHAT COUNTS AS A PROBLEM
- Reuses Cedar Finch Studio, Blue Lantern Supplies, INR 12,000 or INR 24,000.
- Calls the fee monthly, adds an automatic renewal, or invents an extra exit fee.
- Uses the old 30-day cancellation or 60-day renewal notice periods.
- Omits "at least", "written", "both completed and delivered", the pre-final-
  delivery condition, or the payment cap from the relevant explanation.
- Invents a two-year confidentiality duty, liability cap or amendment procedure.
- Cites PDF page 2 or any other page that does not exist in this one-page PDF.
- Shows an old report or old download after switching documents.

HOW TO INTERPRET THE CHECKS
An explicit statement that no additional exit fee applies is information found
in the document. A topic the AI did not identify is a different situation.
Exact quote matches do not prove that the AI's explanations are correct.
Check both wording and meaning. A review or a correction attempt is not proof
of accuracy. Two API requests are normal when no quote correction is needed;
three means the app attempted its one permitted quote correction.
